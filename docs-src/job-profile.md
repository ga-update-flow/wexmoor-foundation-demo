# Program Officer - Job Profile

## About The Wexmoor Foundation

The Wexmoor Foundation is a family foundation that invests in public education, specifically early grades achievement and excellent educators and leaders. It carries forward the commitment to community of the family that founded the Wexmoor Furniture Company. The Foundation makes grants in Brannock County, considers select state-level initiatives in North Carolina, and also funds international programs and organizations.

## About the Program Officer Role

As a member of a small, collaborative team, the Program Officer will contribute strategic thinking to the Foundation's work and will manage the review, due diligence and grant monitoring process for nonprofit partners and school districts working in early literacy and in the teacher and school leader pipeline. The Program Officer will also help raise public awareness of the Foundation's mission and grantees and will support learning opportunities for the Rising Generation and new members of the Board.

The Program Officer will identify and cultivate potential grantees through research, networking and relationship-building across Brannock County and North Carolina. Success in this role requires knowledge of, and a real passion for, public education in Brannock County and across the state. The Program Officer reports to the Executive Director.

## Key Responsibilities

- Manage a portfolio of grants from Letter of Inquiry through final report, including site visits and due diligence
- Prepare clear, concise grant recommendations for the Grants Committee and Board of Directors
- Monitor grantee progress and help partners strengthen their use of data
- Research trends, policy and evidence in early literacy and educator effectiveness
- Plan and support convenings, learning sessions and Board meetings
- Prepare written materials, presentations and content for the website and annual report
- Represent the Foundation in community and statewide education networks

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree required; advanced degree in education, public policy or a related field preferred
- At least five years of experience in education, nonprofit management or philanthropy; classroom or school leadership experience is a plus
- Strong analytical skills and comfort working with student achievement data
- Excellent writing, presentation and relationship-building skills
- Knowledge of public education in North Carolina

## Compensation and Benefits

The Foundation offers a competitive salary commensurate with experience and a comprehensive benefits package, including health insurance, retirement contributions and professional development support. This is a full-time position based in Harlow Springs, North Carolina, with a hybrid schedule.
