Changes from the baseline (eligible applicants: nonprofit organizations, public schools/school districts, private schools,
and colleges and universities):

* Mission & Guidelines, Mission Statement: "invest in public schools and nonprofit organizations" → "invest in public schools,
  private schools and nonprofit organizations".
* Mission & Guidelines, Grant Limitations: removed "Private elementary or secondary schools" (it would contradict the change).
  Individuals, international programs and/or organizations, fundraising, and the 12% F&A policy are unchanged.
* Grant Application Process, Step 1 (also its FAQ post and archive excerpt): "…direct grants to public school districts and to
  colleges and universities" → "…to public school districts, to private schools, and to colleges and universities".
* FAQ "Can we apply if we are not a nonprofit organization?": "No. Only nonprofit organizations, public school districts, and
  colleges and universities may apply for funding." → "Only nonprofit organizations, public school districts, private schools,
  and colleges and universities may apply for funding." (The flat "No." is dropped because a private school that is not a
  nonprofit is now eligible.)
* Eligibility Quiz: Q1 organization type adds "a private school"; Q2 "serve public school students in Brannock County…" →
  "serve public or private school students in Brannock County…" (so eligible private schools are not failed by the
  geographic question; the Brannock County / North Carolina requirement is unchanged); Q4 exclusions no longer list
  "a private elementary or secondary school". Private schools must still pass every other question (geography, focus area,
  exclusions, guidelines reviewed).

Not changed: general statements that the Foundation invests in "public education" (purpose, not applicant eligibility); the
Impact Plan PDF's quotation of the mission statement as adopted in 2011 (dated record); deadlines, Board meetings, contact
details and focus areas. This file is repository documentation only; it is not part of the built website.
