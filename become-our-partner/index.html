Become Our Partner

Illustration: Wexmoor Foundation

The Wexmoor Foundation invests more than dollars. We invest our time, our voice and our energy. We pursue our mission hands-on, spending time in schools and communities and asking good questions of the people who know what works: teachers whose students thrive, principals who have beaten the odds, and leaders of organizations that are exceeding their goals.

We are always looking for organizations that share our passion, our goals and our commitment. The Foundation conducts two grantmaking cycles each year, one in the spring and one in the fall, and final applications are by invitation only. Our process is clear and consistent because, above all, we want to respect your time.

We believe we can help bring about lasting change. We believe that our work, joined with the work of like-minded partners, can set every student on a path to success. And we are confident that the progress we make now will help build a future in which every child has access to an excellent education and to passionate, skilled teachers and leaders.

Grant Cycle Key Dates

January - Invitations for spring grant cycle issued
February - Spring applications due
May - Spring grant decisions communicated
July - Invitations for fall grant cycle issued
August - Fall applications due
October/November - Fall grant decisions communicated
