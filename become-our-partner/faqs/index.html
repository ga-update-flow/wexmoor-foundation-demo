FAQs

We do not make grants to individuals. The Wexmoor Foundation has endowed scholarship funds at several North Carolina colleges and universities, but it is not able to award scholarships to individual students.

No. The Wexmoor Foundation makes grants only within the United States, and grants are not awarded to international programs and/or organizations. Please see the Geographic Focus section of our Mission & Guidelines.

Yes. Our spring application deadline is in February and our fall application deadline is in August; however, the Foundation accepts applications only from organizations it has invited to apply. Please review our Application Process.

The Wexmoor Foundation awards most of its grants to organizations identified by our staff and board. Please review our Application Process to learn how to introduce your organization to the Foundation.

No. Only nonprofit organizations, public school districts, and colleges and universities may apply for funding.

In some circumstances you may apply through a fiscal sponsor. The fiscal sponsor must be a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization with a formal relationship to the organization carrying out the project. In these cases the grant is made directly to the fiscal sponsor, which is responsible for the proper use of funds and for all reporting requirements.

The Silas Wexmoor Trust is a separate private foundation that supports workforce training and community college pathways for adult learners. It was established in 1988 by Silas Wexmoor Jr. and has its own board, staff and grantmaking process.

The Wexmoor Foundation is a family foundation established by the founders of the Wexmoor Furniture Company. The Foundation has always been a separate entity from the company, which was sold in 2015 and has no role in the Foundation’s governance or grantmaking.

No, the Foundation does not sponsor fundraising events.

The Wexmoor Foundation does not provide merchandise or donated items.

It is the policy of The Wexmoor Foundation Board of Directors to allow up to 12% of a grant to an institution of higher education to be used for indirect overhead expenses, or “F&A.” A copy of this policy can be found here.
