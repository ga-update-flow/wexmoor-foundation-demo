Grant Application Process

We partner with nonprofit organizations holding 501(c)(3) status, and we also make direct grants to public school districts, to private schools, and to colleges and universities. Our resources are dedicated to Early Grades Achievement and Educators & Leaders, locally in Brannock County and at the state level across North Carolina. To judge whether your organization is a strong fit, we encourage you to review the following:

Our approach
Our guidelines
Outcomes sought
Past grantees

If, after reviewing these materials, you see clear alignment between your organization’s work and our mission, please complete the Foundation’s Eligibility Quiz.

Organizations that meet the basic requirements in the Eligibility Quiz will be taken to a Letter of Inquiry form, which asks for:

  • Contact information
  • Organization’s federal Tax ID (EIN)
  • A short description of how your organization’s purpose and/or program advances the mission of the Foundation
  • Geographic area served

Within a few weeks of receiving your inquiry, we will let you know whether your organization or program appears to align with the Foundation’s desired outcomes. Please note that an initial expression of interest does not guarantee that your organization will be invited to apply for funding.

If your organization is selected to submit a full application, a member of the Foundation staff will contact you. Because we are focused on results, our application asks you to describe the specific results your organization commits to achieving and the evidence you will use to measure progress. Please refer to our Outcomes Sought document as you prepare the final application, particularly when describing how you will measure success. The Foundation conducts two grantmaking cycles each year—one in the spring and one in the fall. Spring cycle applications are issued in January, and fall cycle applications are issued in July.

Final applications are reviewed by the Grants Committee and then presented to the Board of Directors for approval. The Board meets in April and in October/November to make final funding decisions. Foundation staff will notify applicants once funding decisions have been confirmed.
