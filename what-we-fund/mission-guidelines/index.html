Mission & Guidelines

The Wexmoor Foundation Mission Statement

The Wexmoor Foundation believes every child deserves a strong start and skilled, caring educators. Our mission is to invest in public schools, private schools and nonprofit organizations that help every student read with confidence by the end of third grade and learn from excellent teachers and school leaders throughout their K-12 years, so that all students graduate ready for college, career and life.

Foundations for Success

An excellent education is a core value to which we are fully committed. Research and experience show that early grades achievement and excellent educators and leaders are the building blocks of a successful education. We invest deeply in these two areas to help bring about system-wide change that ensures a high-quality education for every student. Because we believe all children can succeed, the gap in achievement between students from low-income families and their more affluent peers can, and must, be closed. That is why we focus our resources on the students who need them most, and why we build relationships with high-performing organizations and results-focused school systems.

Outcomes Sought

To give grantseekers more guidance, we offer The Foundation’s Outcomes Sought document, which describes the kinds of outcomes we hope to see from our investments. It is not meant to be exhaustive. Instead, it is a starting point for conversation as your organization considers its alignment with the Foundation’s priorities or prepares a final application.

Geographic Focus

The Wexmoor Foundation makes grants to improve public education for the students of Brannock County. The Foundation also considers select state-level initiatives in North Carolina that align with our focus areas and show evidence of, or strong potential for, large-scale impact.

In the past, the Foundation also made grants in Upstate South Carolina and Southside Virginia. In 2017, the Board chose to concentrate its giving in Brannock County and North Carolina. We remain grateful to the many partners and educators in those communities from whom we learned so much.

Grant Limitations

Grants from The Wexmoor Foundation are not awarded to:

  • Individuals
  • International programs and/or organizations
  • Organizations for the primary purpose of fundraising. This includes sponsoring events or providing door prizes, gift cards, merchandise or other giveaways.

It is the policy of The Wexmoor Foundation Board of Directors to allow up to 12% of a grant to an institution of higher education to be used for indirect overhead expenses, or “F&A.” A copy of this policy can be found here.
