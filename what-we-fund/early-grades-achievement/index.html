Early Grades Achievement

Illustration: Wexmoor Foundation

The years from kindergarten through third grade are when children learn to read. Every year after that, they read to learn.

— The Wexmoor Foundation Impact Plan

A strong early foundation is essential to future success in school. During the formative years from kindergarten through third grade, children build the foundational skills, and the love of learning, that carry them through school. Within our Early Grades Achievement focus area, we look for partners who help students perform on or above grade level in core subjects by the end of third grade, starting in kindergarten.

Outcomes Sought

We support organizations that measure their results and use evidence-based strategies. In Early Grades Achievement, we pay particular attention to student growth as well as improved test scores. Our FY 2027 Outcomes Sought document summarizes our priorities and serves as a basis for conversation with grantees. We welcome a dialogue with grantseekers about their approach and how they use data.

Strategic Issue:
Early Reading Proficiency

In Brannock County, the Foundation’s goal, in partnership with Read Brannock, is to increase the percentage of third graders reading at College and Career Ready levels on the state reading assessment from 31% in spring 2023 to 75% by spring 2031.

Alongside our local strategy, at the North Carolina state level, the Foundation supports research, policy and effective practice to raise third grade reading proficiency. A particular priority is making sure every elementary teacher has the knowledge and skill to teach reading to every learner.

Strategies

  • Provide individualized support through evidence-based tutoring
  • Prevent summer learning loss through high-quality summer reading programs
  • Strengthen teacher knowledge and instruction in evidence-based reading practices
  • Help families and caregivers support reading at home and connect to community resources
  • Ensure that systems and policies are informed by reading research and help students, families and educators reach the third grade proficiency goal
Explore the Strategic Issue Explore the Full Strategy and Impact Plan

What’s New

The Foundation remains a committed lead funder of Read Brannock, which it helped launch in 2016, and continues to support strong state-level policy and system practices aligned with reading research. The Foundation’s current Impact Plan, adopted in 2019 and revised in 2025, named a second strategic issue, strong teachers for every classroom, to sharpen the Educators & Leaders focus area, just as the focus on early reading sharpens Early Grades Achievement.

Data That Shapes Our Approach

  • Students who are not reading proficiently by the end of third grade are significantly less likely to graduate from high school on time.
  • In spring 2023, 31% of Brannock County Schools third graders scored at College and Career Ready levels on the state reading assessment.
  • Among Brannock County third graders from economically disadvantaged families, fewer than one in four reached College and Career Ready levels in spring 2023.
  • Nearly all students, regardless of background, can learn to read when they receive enough explicit, systematic instruction in foundational reading skills.
