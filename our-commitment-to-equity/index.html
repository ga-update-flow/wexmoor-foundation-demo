Our Commitment to Equity and Belonging

As a foundation dedicated to an excellent public education for every student, The Wexmoor Foundation is committed to making sure that our learning and our actions reflect equity and belonging. This commitment makes our work more relevant, more credible and more effective, and it strengthens our ability to fulfill our mission.

We seek out a wide range of perspectives, backgrounds and experiences among the people we listen to, learn from and invest in. We work toward policies and practices that give children of every background a genuine opportunity to thrive, and we strive to create an environment where our staff, board, volunteers and partners are respected and heard.

This is ongoing work. Each year the Board and staff review how our grantmaking, our convenings and our own operations reflect these commitments, and we share what we learn in our annual report.
