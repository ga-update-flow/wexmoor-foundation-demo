Annual Reports

We are pleased to share our FY 2026 Annual Report, a look back at a year of progress, partnership and shared commitment to the students of Brannock County and North Carolina. This year we marked ten years of Read Brannock, the community literacy effort the Foundation helped launch, and we deepened our work to prepare and support excellent teachers for the classrooms that need them most. The report reflects the dedication of the teachers, principals, tutors, families and partner organizations who make this work possible. We invite you to explore the FY 2026 Report and learn more about what is happening in classrooms across our community and our state.
