About the Foundation

Strong Starts. Lasting Results.

The Wexmoor Foundation invests in public education, with a clear focus on early grades achievement, on excellent educators and school leaders and on extracurricular activities, the three foundations on which every student’s success is built.

As a private family foundation, we look for partners who share our sense of urgency. Our support goes beyond dollars: we give our time, our voice and our convening power. We learn by listening to the people closest to students—teachers whose classrooms consistently outperform expectations, principals who have turned schools around, and leaders of organizations that are meeting ambitious goals. We build relationships with high-performing organizations and results-focused school systems because we believe that, working together, we can help every child read, learn and lead.

Our Commitment to Equity and Belonging

As a foundation dedicated to an excellent public education for every student, The Wexmoor Foundation is committed to equity in how we learn, how we work and where we invest. You can read more about how we reflect, learn and improve here.
