Financials

The Wexmoor Foundation fiscal year is July 1 - June 30.

As a private foundation, the Foundation files a Form 990-PF with the IRS each year. Please contact us with any questions about our tax return.

Fiscal Year 2025 (July 1, 2024 - June 30, 2025)

Total assets were $57,316,402. Grants distributed were $2,412,750.
