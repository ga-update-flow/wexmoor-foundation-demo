Our History

A Tradition of Giving

For more than ninety years, the Wexmoor family has supported schools, congregations, hospitals and civic organizations in the North Carolina Piedmont. Based in Harlow Springs, The Wexmoor Foundation carries forward the commitment to community begun by brothers Ambrose and Silas Wexmoor, who founded the Wexmoor Furniture Company in 1909.

The Ambrose Wexmoor Memorial Fund was established by the company in 1931 following Ambrose Wexmoor’s death. The fund first helped rebuild schoolhouses and libraries in the mill villages around Harlow Springs, and over time it supported a wide range of health, education and community causes.

In 1957, after the death of Silas Wexmoor, his heirs joined the charitable portion of his estate with the Memorial Fund to create The Wexmoor Foundation. Today the Foundation remains a family foundation that looks to its founders for inspiration and seeks to express the family’s values through its charitable investments.

Mr. Wexmoor knew the name of every person on the factory floor and the names of their children. He used to say that a town is only as strong as its schools, and he meant it.

— Harold Pettibone, retired Wexmoor Furniture plant manager and former Foundation board member

For many years, the Wexmoor Furniture Company made annual contributions to the Foundation in support of its charitable work. The company was sold in 2015, and the Foundation has always operated as a separate entity, rooted in the same family history of care for the people of the Piedmont.

In 2011, the Board of Directors adopted a new mission statement that placed public education at the center of the Foundation’s work. To deepen the impact of its giving, the Foundation now invests only in programs within its focus areas: early grades achievement, excellent educators and leaders, and extracurricular activities.
