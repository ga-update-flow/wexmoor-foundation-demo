The Wexmoor Foundation works every day toward lasting change in our focus areas. Check back often for the latest news and announcements.

Read Brannock Marks Ten Years of Community Literacy Work

Ten years after The Wexmoor Foundation convened community leaders around a simple question, how many of our third graders can read, Read Brannock has grown into a county-wide network of schools, libraries, families and volunteers.

Brannock County Schools Expands Literacy Coaching Network

Brannock County Schools announced that every elementary school in the district will have access to a trained literacy coach beginning in the 2025-26 school year.

Dr. Renee Callahan Named Executive Director of The Wexmoor Foundation
