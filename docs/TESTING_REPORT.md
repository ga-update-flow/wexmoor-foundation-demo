## PDFs

All 12 PDFs are text-based (generated by ReportLab, not images) with Title/Author/Subject metadata and
`en-US` language. Text extraction verified with pypdf: Outcomes Sought (4 pp), Indirect Cost Policy (1 p),
Impact Plan (7 pp, landscape), Early Reading strategic issue (1 p, landscape), Logic Model (1 p, landscape),
FY2025 Financial Summary (1 p), Program Officer job profile (2 pp), annual reports FY2022–FY2026 (4 pp each).
ReportLab does not emit tagged-PDF structure trees. The documents are readable and selectable but not
fully PDF/UA tagged.

## Information presence and absence (scan of built HTML)

* Dollar figures appear only where the reference has equivalents: annual giving ($2.4 million, About
  page) and the Financials page (total assets, grants distributed).
* Deadlines are month-level only (Become Our Partner key dates; FAQ "February … August"). No day-level dates.
* No "Apply Now", award range, award amount, grant/project period, LOI deadline, "coming soon" or
  "not available" text anywhere.
* Full application is invitation-only (Step 3, Become Our Partner, FAQ). The only public form is the LOI behind
  the eligibility quiz, mirroring the reference.

## Consistency checks

* Annual report focus-area totals sum to grants paid for every year; grant counts equal the number of
  grantee rows in the corresponding cycle pages (FY2026: 9 + 12 = 21; FY2025: 8 + 8 = 16; FY2024: 7 + 7 = 14).
* FY2025 grants distributed ($2,412,750) matches across Financials page, financial-summary PDF and FY2025 annual report.
* Indirect-cost cap 12% matches across Mission & Guidelines, FAQ and the policy PDF.
* Cycle months match across Become Our Partner, Step 3, Step 4 and FAQ (applications issued Jan/Jul,
  due Feb/Aug, Board meets Apr and Oct/Nov).
* Reading goal (31% in spring 2023 → 75% by spring 2031) matches across the Early Grades page, Impact Plan and one-pager.

## Known limitations

* The live site is an anonymous Netlify deploy, so it is password-gated (HTTP 401) until claimed, and Netlify
  deletes it if it isn't claimed within 60 minutes of deployment.
* `_headers` was uploaded and consumed by Netlify, but no `X-Robots-Tag` header was observed on the unclaimed deploy.
  Re-check after claiming (`curl -sI <url>/uploads/2025/02/Outcomes-Sought-by-The-Wexmoor-Foundation.pdf`).
  If it is still missing, redeploy from the repo with `netlify.toml`, which also declares the header.
* `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and canonical links were built for `https://wexmoor-foundation-demo.netlify.app`.
  Rename the claimed site to `wexmoor-foundation-demo`, or rebuild with `SITE_URL=<actual URL>`.
